UNDERSTANDING THE SITUATION

The History of
Xenophobia in South Africa

Over the years, foreign nationals, including many Nigerians, have faced discrimination, attacks and displacement in South Africa. This section highlights key incidents, the human impact, and the ongoing need for support, protection and humanitarian intervention.

Learn More About the Crisis ›
1994 – 2007

Early Incidents

Reports documented hostility and violence toward migrants in the post-apartheid period.

2008

Major Wave of Violence

A widespread outbreak caused deaths, injuries and large-scale displacement.

2015

Renewed Attacks

Attacks in KwaZulu-Natal and other areas again displaced foreign nationals.

2019

Another Major Wave

Violence and looting affected foreign-owned businesses and communities.

2021 – Present

Ongoing Challenges

Human-rights groups continue documenting anti-migrant harassment and violence.
