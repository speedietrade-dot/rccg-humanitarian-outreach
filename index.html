ABOUT US

The Redeemed Christian Church of God

The Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG) is a global Christian movement committed to the holistic well-being of people. Through our humanitarian outreach, we provide support, relief and hope to Nigerians in distress, both at home and abroad.

THE CRISIS

Understanding the Situation in South Africa

Many Nigerians living in South Africa have faced violence, displacement and discrimination. Our humanitarian mission seeks to provide immediate relief, safe evacuation, and long-term support for those affected.

Learn More About The Crisis  ›
Violence and Attacks

Nigerians have faced targeted violence in some communities.

Displacement

Many have been forced to leave their homes and businesses.

Need for Support

Urgent evacuation, shelter and reintegration are needed.
